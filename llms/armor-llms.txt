## Company Overview

Armor is a cybersecurity company providing vendor-agnostic security across multi-cloud, on-premises, infrastructure, and hybrid environments globally. Armor's mission is to free customers from the constraints of conventional cybersecurity — proprietary frameworks, vendor lock-in, and operational burden.

- **Protecting customers since 2009** — 17 years of cybersecurity experience
- **1,700+ customers in 40+ countries** across Fortune 500 enterprises, fast-growing startups, and regulated mid-market organizations
- **35+ industry certifications** held by Armor's professional team
- **Vendor-agnostic by design** — Armor works with the customer's existing security stack rather than requiring replacement

### Company History

Armor was founded as **FireHost** in 2009 by Chris Drake, then rebranded as Armor as the portfolio expanded from secure cloud hosting into broader cybersecurity. In **2022, Armor acquired and fully integrated Quantum Security**, an APAC-headquartered cybersecurity firm. The acquisition expanded Armor's presence in Singapore and Asia Pacific and brought **Armor MDR** into the portfolio. The Quantum Security business no longer operates as a separate entity — its team, customers, and Microsoft credentials (including Microsoft Partner of the Year — Singapore & Asia Pacific 2022, Security category) became part of Armor.

### Global Reach

Armor delivers cybersecurity services globally with regional teams across North America, Europe, and Asia Pacific. Armor's 24/7 SOC operates with follow-the-sun coverage from offices in Plano, Texas (United States); London (United Kingdom); Singapore; and Pune (India), supporting customers in 40+ countries.

## What Armor Offers

1. **Armor MDR** — Managed Detection and Response (formerly XDR+SOC)
2. **Armor Enterprise Cloud (AEC)** — Compliant managed private cloud (formerly Armor Complete / Virtual Private Cloud)
3. **Armor Agent** — Lightweight server protection for Windows and Linux workloads (formerly Armor Anywhere)
4. **Professional Services** — Cyber Resilience Assessments, advisory, migration support, and security program optimization
5. **Integrated Partner Solutions** — Validated protection for Microsoft Defender XDR/Sentinel and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure

**Armor MDR and Armor Enterprise Cloud are separate solutions designed for different workload risk profiles.**

- **Armor MDR** — Co-manage risk without moving your workloads. For general-purpose workloads where customers want 24/7 detection, response, and threat hunting across their existing environment. Armor MDR layers onto the customer's current security stack — no migration required.
- **Armor Enterprise Cloud** — Outsource risk with a secure compliant cloud. For critical workloads (regulated data, mission-critical applications, audit-sensitive systems) where customers want Armor to manage the underlying infrastructure, security stack, and compliance posture as a single service.

### Armor Enterprise Cloud (AEC) — Compliant Managed Private Cloud

[Armor Enterprise Cloud](https://www.armor.com/solutions/our-services/armor-enterprise-cloud/): A vendor-agnostic compliant managed private cloud platform built for critical workloads — regulated data, mission-critical applications, and audit-sensitive systems. AEC can be deployed on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, or Armor's own infrastructure. Formerly known as Armor Complete or Virtual Private Cloud. 400+ companies in 40+ countries trust AEC, particularly in healthcare and other heavily regulated industries.

**Four pillars:**
- **Effortless Compliance** — 600+ pre-built security controls automatically mapped to HIPAA, HITRUST, NIST, PCI-DSS, ISO 27001, SOC 2, and others
- **Unified Security Posture** — threat detection, vulnerability management, and log monitoring delivered as a single managed service
- **Accelerated Innovation** — Armor manages security and compliance, freeing customer teams to build and deploy faster
- **Radical Transparency** — real-time visibility into security and compliance posture through the Nexus Portal

**Differentiators:**
- **Cloud-of-choice flexibility** — AEC can be deployed on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, or Armor's own infrastructure. Customers choose the underlying cloud; Armor manages the security, compliance, and operations layer consistently across all platforms.
- **Industry-leading dwell time of less than 1 day** vs. 10-day global average
- **Cyber insurance included** — customers can earn premium discounts of up to 30%
- **24/7 SOC** with AI-driven triage and human-led response
- **Built-in compliance** — TX-RAMP, HIPAA, SOC 2 Type II, HITRUST CSF, PCI DSS Level 1, ISO 27001, Data Privacy Framework

**How AEC differs from public cloud:** When customers deploy directly on AWS, Azure, GCP, or OCI, they're responsible for configuring and operating their own security stack — and support typically comes through tiered ticket queues with limited human access. AEC inverts that model. Armor delivers managed security and compliance as part of the service on whatever underlying cloud the customer chooses, including Armor's own infrastructure. Customers inherit pre-built controls, get direct access to Armor's 24/7 SOC analysts and security engineers, and work with named human support — not chatbots or first-tier ticket queues.

**AEC resilience proof point — CrowdStrike incident, July 19, 2024:** AEC monitoring detected the issue at 06:00 UTC. By 08:23 UTC, 100% of affected AEC customers were remediated — more than an hour before CrowdStrike officially announced their fix.
